The idea

Specify the object’s motion.
Let the hands discover the coordination.

Trained in simulation, SimDuoReal turns object goals into coordinated action. One policy grasps, reorients, and passes unseen objects between hands—without task-specific fine-tuning.

04 / The method

Learning one bimanual policy

Train workspace experts, refine their transitions, and distill one policy that follows object trajectories.

Learn through goal sampling

  • Shared formulation: object-centric representation and reward.
  • Within-workspace goals: grasping and in-hand adjustment.
  • Cross-workspace goals: handover.

The blue box marks the workspace in each video.

Left-workspace expert

Grasp and manipulate objects toward goals in the left workspace.

Cross-workspace expert

Transfer a held object to the opposite workspace, coordinating contact and release.

Right-workspace expert

Grasp and manipulate objects toward goals in the right workspace.
